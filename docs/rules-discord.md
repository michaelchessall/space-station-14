# Discord Rules:
## Rule 1: Respect and Courtesy
1.1 - Treat all members with respect and courtesy. Toxicity, harassment, and impersonation of others are explicitly forbidden.
1.2 - Engage in constructive and positive discussions, avoiding personal attacks or offensive language, or bigoted content.
## Rule 2: Content Restrictions
2.1 - Do not post or share shock content, including pornography, explicit 18+ material, illegal activities, or erotic written materials.
2.2 - Refrain from sharing spoilers about recent events in-game unless specified otherwise for the channel.
## Rule 3: Advertising and External Content
3.1 - Avoid advertising content or requesting reactions to content, such as applications,  other servers, etc, unless explicitly permitted by staff in the channel.
3.2 - Keep discussions focused on the relevant channels, or threads.
## Rule 4: Recent In-Game Events
4.1 - Do not post information or discuss recent events OOCly (included in this Discord) unless you have staff approval or they follow in-game guidelines to allow it.
4.2 - Respect the guidelines for discussing events and information.
## Rule 5: Mentioning and Communication
5.1 - Refrain from @mentioning users, roles, or groups without a valid reason.
## Rule 6: Spam and Inappropriate Behavior
6.1 - Do not engage in spamming or flood the channels with repetitive or excessive messages.
6.2 - Avoid discussing topics that are inappropriate for the server, likely to result in rule violations, or suggestive in nature.
